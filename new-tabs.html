Key News & Trends Impacting RACV Resorts
8 SIGNALS
⛽
Fuel crisis impact on regional travel: Remote/distant regions seeing up to −20% visitation, however proximal destinations (Mornington Peninsula, Yarra Valley, Great Ocean Road corridor) remain solid. RACV Torquay, Cape Schanck and Healesville well-positioned; Cobram more exposed.
🚌
Free VIC public transport April 2026: State government announcement creates significant day-trip and short-break opportunity for resorts within reach of Melbourne. Healesville (Yarra Valley Rail Trail) and Mornington Peninsula (via Frankston line) can activate specific campaign creative targeting non-drivers.
💰
Regional travellers spend +$286 per trip: Boomtown data shows domestic regional travellers outspend metro travellers by $286 per trip on average. RACV Resorts positioned to capture high-yield regional segments with bundled packages (golf + spa + dining).
🧖
Wellness travel boom: 58% of high-income Australians plan a wellness trip in 2026. Strong tailwind for RACV Healesville (One Spa, wine region, mountain setting) and RACV Cape Schanck (One Spa, ocean views, championship golf). Both should amplify spa + wellness messaging in paid and organic channels.
🏗️
Inverloch expansion — 36 new rooms under construction: Capacity uplift coming to RACV Inverloch. Key opportunity to pre-sell launch packages for the new room block, drive early reviews at opening, and position Inverloch as the premier coastal escape on the South Gippsland Coast.
✈️
Qantas Hobart base closure risk: Qantas network restructuring threatens air access to Hobart. RACV Hobart Hotel relies on interstate fly-in guests. Risk of reduced demand needs mitigation — drive market partnership, Tasmanian road trip packaging, and Rex/Jetstar partnerships should be explored urgently.
📶
Cobram WiFi complaint — recurring pain point: Guest reviews consistently flag no WiFi in cabins and caravan sites at RACV Cobram as a top-1 complaint. In an era of remote work and always-on connectivity, this is a booking barrier particularly for families and working travellers. Infrastructure investment recommended.
🐣
Easter 2026 results — mixed by geography: Mornington Peninsula (Cape Schanck) reported solid occupancy; remote areas (Cobram) down ~20%. Proximity to Melbourne emerging as the strongest predictor of Easter performance for RACV Resorts. Portfolio tilt toward proximal properties validates strategy.

Key Conference & Events Trends 2026
6 SIGNALS
💻
Hybrid events now standard expectation: In-person + virtual attendance is now expected for major conferences. RACV City Club's LED walls and streaming capability provide a competitive advantage. Cape Schanck and resort venues should invest in hybrid technology to capture the delegate audience beyond the physical room.
🌿
Sustainability requirements growing: Corporate event planners increasingly require carbon-neutral venue credentials, waste reduction commitments, and plant-based menu options. RACV should develop and publish a formal Event Sustainability Policy across all venues to win ESG-sensitive corporate clients.
🍷
Experiential add-ons now a decision driver: Wine tastings (Healesville), spa packages (Cape Schanck, Healesville), golf experiences (all resorts) and coastal activities (Torquay, Inverloch) are primary differentiators for corporate retreat planners choosing between venue options. RACV resorts lead on experiential breadth.
📺
Technology expectations escalating: Planners expect LED walls, real-time streaming, touch-enabled AV, app-based audience engagement and seamless WiFi across all event spaces. Venues without upgraded AV packages face deselection at shortlisting stage. Regular tech refresh cycle required.
🏞️
Regional retreat demand post-COVID structural: Corporate teams increasingly prefer off-site regional retreats over CBD venues for strategy days and leadership programs — seeking immersion, networking, and reduced digital distraction. RACV Resort network (Cape Schanck, Healesville, Torquay) uniquely positioned to capture this structural demand shift.
📊
MICE sector — $270M to Victoria in 2025: Melbourne Convention Bureau data shows 230 events secured in 2025, contributing approximately $270M to the Victorian economy. RACV's multi-venue network (CBD + regional resorts) positions it to capture both CBD MICE and regional offsite segments. MCB partnership should be formalised.
