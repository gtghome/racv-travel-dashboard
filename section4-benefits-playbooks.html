81 Victorian Petrol Stations Run Dry — Supply Chain Disruption

A logistics disruption at the Geelong refinery and downstream distribution network caused 81 Victorian petrol stations to run out of fuel on April 3, 2026 — the largest domestic fuel stockout event since 2022. The event lasted 18–36 hours and disproportionately affected outer suburban and regional areas. RACV Roadside received a 34% spike in calls related to fuel and breakdown on the day. The RACV fuel discount network's real-time station availability data proved critical, with the RACV app recording its highest single-day traffic in 3 years as members checked live availability.

Transport Policy Apr 2026
Free VIC Public Transport — April Holiday Period Reduces Private Car Dependence

The Victorian Government's free public transport initiative during the April school holiday period (April 12–27, 2026) reduced private vehicle trips into Melbourne's CBD by an estimated 18% over the period.
