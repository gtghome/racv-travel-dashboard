Strategic Recommendations — Packages & Themes 6 Priority Actions
1
Lead with Volume: Coastal Retreat + Family Adventure = 2 × “Very High” themes P1 — Q2 2026
Comfortable Communities and Establishing Roots represent the largest member volume. A targeted “Coastal VIC” school-holiday campaign anchored at Flinders Hotel and Drift House, with a QLD option via JW Marriott, will generate the highest total booking volume of any theme campaign.
2
Mosaic-Triggered EDM Calendar: 12 monthly drops mapped to segments and seasons P1 — Q2 2026
Build a 12-month Mosaic-triggered EDM calendar. Suburban Stability and Senior Security respond to midweek offers year-round — serve them Wellness & Wine. Rising Prosperity needs Thursday flash drops. Comfortable Communities needs 6-week-before-school-holiday cadence. One segmented calendar drives 3× the bookings of a single broadcast.
3
Senior Security Midweek Fill Programme: 6–10 trips/year is untapped P1 — Ongoing
Senior Security are RACV’s highest frequency domestic travellers (6–10 trips/year) with weekday flexibility. Partner with Craig’s Royal, Peppers Hepburn and MACq 01 on 3-for-2 midweek deals distributed via RACV magazine and outbound phone. This segment fills occupancy gaps competitors cannot access.
4
Rising Prosperity Flash Deal Engine: Thursday → Weekend pipeline P2 — Q3 2026
Build a Thursday afternoon flash deal engine targeting Rising Prosperity members via push notification and Instagram. QT Melbourne, Crown Metropol and QT Gold Coast unsold weekend inventory can be converted at 48-hour notice. This segment impulse-books and is under-served by current RACV communications cadence.
5
Develop Goldfields Heritage Circuit Package with V/Line Integration P2 — Q4 2026
Modest Traditions and Senior Security segments are car-light or prefer train. Partner with V/Line for a Ballarat–Bendigo–Castlemaine circuit package including Craig’s Royal accommodation and curated heritage walks. Distributed via RACV magazine, direct mail and local radio — channels that reach these segments where others do not.
6
TI Cross-Sell at Package Checkout: Convert hotel bookers to travel insurance buyers P1 — Q2 2026
Every hotel + flight package checkout is a natural TI conversion moment. Domestic TI is under-penetrated vs international. Affluent Achievers (Saffire Freycinet, Ovolo) should see annual multi-trip TI. Comfortable Communities (JW Marriott GC family break) should see family domestic TI. Segment-matched TI placement at checkout expected to lift TI attachment rate 15–25%.
